### Selected Engineering Projects & R&D

#### 1. Digital IC & FPGA Hardware Acceleration
* **Edge-LLM GEMV Hardware Accelerator (PERURI Chip Hackathon 2026):**  
  Designed an FPGA-based matrix-vector multiplication accelerator targeting the **Terasic DE10-Nano (Intel Cyclone V SoC)**. Implemented an 8-way SIMD INT8 Vector-MAC (`vector_mac_simd8`), a hardware CRC-8 weight integrity engine (`crc8_engine`) for bit-flip detection, and a GEMV FSM controller verified bit-exactly against a Python golden model.
* **Tiny Tapeout 7 (TT07) Digital Design:**  
  Developed and verified digital RTL modules including an Iterative Multiply-Accumulate (`tt07_iterativeMAC`) core for open-source ASIC fabrication.

#### 2. UAV Propulsion & Avionics Hardware (GAMAFORCE UGM)
* **80A Sensorless BLDC Electronic Speed Controller (ESC):**  
  Led the end-to-end R&D of custom UAV propulsion controllers. Designed schematics and multi-layer PCBs integrating ATmega328P, L6388ED/IR2101 gate drivers, AON6312 MOSFETs, and BTN7971 bridges with active thermal management and overcurrent protection.
* **Custom UAV Flight Controller & 64-Channel Multiplexer PCB:**  
  Engineered avionics control boards and low-noise 64-channel data acquisition multiplexers for multi-sensor instrumentation.

#### 3. Industrial & Agricultural Instrumentation
* **Portable Soil NPK Nutrient Analyzer (Prawira Tani & PT Patmanunggal Reka Abadi):**  
  Engineered a complete portable soil nutrient analyzer featuring custom PCBs (Mainboard, Battery Management System, RS485 Sensor Interface, SD Card/RS232 Logger), modular OOP firmware, sensor system identification/calibration, Nextion HMI interface, and 3D-printed enclosure.
* **IoT Smart Greenhouse & Automated Feeder (PT Helber Elektronika Indonesia & FRC UGM):**  
  Developed real-time environmental monitoring and control systems integrating pH, EC/PPM, temperature, humidity, and light sensors, alongside full 3D mechanical greenhouse modeling in Autodesk Inventor.
